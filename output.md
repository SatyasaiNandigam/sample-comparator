Product summary
Horizon 7 is a single-premium deferred fixed annuity designed for
individuals seeking tax-deferred accumulation and a predictable
initial interest rate over a seven-year guarantee period. The contract
is funded with one accepted premium and does not accept
additional premiums after issue. Interest is credited under a fixed
account; no indexed or variable investment options are offered.
The contract combines a stated surrender-charge schedule with a
market value adjustment (MVA), annual liquidity provisions, a base
death benefit and limited event-based surrender-charge waivers. It
is not designed as an immediate income annuity and does not
include guaranteed living-benefit riders in this illustrative
configuration.

Market Value Adjustment
A market value adjustment (MVA) applies in addition to the
surrender charge for transactions subject to the MVA during the
surrender-charge period. Depending on the change in market
interest rates and the contract's MVA formula, the adjustment may
be positive or negative. A positive adjustment may increase
proceeds; a negative adjustment may reduce proceeds. The
adjustment is calculated under the contract and is not a separately
stated recurring fee.
Transaction considerations
A full surrender may be subject to both the applicable surrender
charge and an MVA, unless a contractual exception or waiver
applies.
Partial withdrawals may reduce contract value and may affect
future accumulation and benefits.
Penalty-free withdrawals and qualifying event-based waivers are
described separately and should not be conflated with the standard
surrender schedule.
OWNER DISCLOSURE Before a withdrawal, the contract owner
should receive an illustration of the estimated surrender value,
including any surrender charge and applicable MVA, under the
terms and valuation date used by the insurer.

Base Contract Benefits & Access
Benefits available under the base contract, including beneficiary
proceeds and liquidity exceptions.

Penalty-free withdrawals
Beginning after the first contract anniversary, the owner may
withdraw up to 10% of the contract's value as measured on the
prior contract anniversary during each contract year without a
surrender charge or MVA. Amounts above the free-withdrawal limit
may be subject to the standard surrender provisions. The precise
calculation, timing and treatment of prior withdrawals are
controlled by the issued contract.

<!-- Page 18 -->

Base death benefit
If the owner dies before annuitization, the beneficiary is entitled to
the contract's full accumulation value, subject to the contract's
beneficiary settlement provisions and applicable law. In this
example, no surrender charge or MVA is applied to proceeds
payable because of the owner's death. This is the base contract
death benefit and is not a guaranteed minimum death benefit rider.
